# Project Overview
## **Smart Warehouse Management System** 
## 1. Introduction

Smart Warehouse Management System (WMS) is an intelligent cloud-based Warehouse Management System to assist distributors and manufacturers in digitizing their warehouse operations. Designed based on enterprise-grade operational standards, this advanced software optimizes inventory control, stock visibility, and the flow of products from the receiving phase to the delivering phase. Smart WMS also minimizes errors, enhances traceability, and provides users with accurate stock information at all times by substituting automated workflows, barcode/RFID scanning, and real-time inventory tracking for spreadsheets and manual stock counting.

## 2. Project Background

In the era of e-commerce and modern supply chains, Warehouse Management System is no longer a storage facility; it is the "heart" that determines the operational speed and costs of the entire enterprise. However, the majority of small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) currently operate their warehouses using traditional methods, leading to serious **bottlenecks**:

- **Over-reliance on manual tools (Excel & ledgers)**:
 Easily exposed by human error (e.g., typos in codes, misaligned rows, failure to update).

- **Information asymmetry**: Excel lacks real-time update capabilities. When an employee is editing a file on their machine, other departments cannot see the current inventory levels.

- **Lack of audit trail**: It is difficult to trace who processed specific stock movements or when they occurred in the event of inventory loss.
